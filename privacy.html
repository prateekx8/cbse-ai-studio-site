Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

CBSE AI Studio (“the app”) is a tool run by its owner on their own computer to publish educational videos to the owner's YouTube channel. This policy explains what Google user data the app uses.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests the YouTube scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). It uses it only to read the signed-in channel's ID, create and update playlists, upload videos, and add videos to playlists on that channel.

How data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens, the channel ID and the IDs of playlists the app creates are stored in local configuration files on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local configuration files removes the stored tokens.
