CBSE AI Studio

CBSE AI Studio is a tool for making short animated video lessons for the CBSE (NCERT) Science and Mathematics syllabus, Classes 6 to 12, and publishing them on the prateek2talk YouTube channel.

It signs in with Google only to manage that channel: it creates one playlist per class and subject and one per chapter, uploads lesson videos, and adds each video to its playlists.
